The Intelligence Pipeline.
How NotesAI processes lectures.

01

Transcript Extraction

When you input a YouTube link, our backend immediately interfaces with the video to extract its closed-captioning data natively. We parse thousands of words of spoken content in milliseconds.

02

NLP Deep Processing

The raw transcript isn't useful on its own. We run it through heavy NLTK pipelines to tokenize sentences, remove conversational filler ("um", "like"), and use neural models (LSTMs) alongside TF-IDF Vectorization to score the most important lines in the video.

03

Semantic Categorization

The distilled, high-value sentences are then mapped. Definitions go to 'Summary', examples to 'Key Learnings', and core formulas to 'Concepts'. The output is sent back to your Dashboard ready for downloading or Flashcard-studying.
